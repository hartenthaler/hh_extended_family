## Extended Family 2.2.6.15

- Adds an administrator-controlled baptism/Christening fallback for missing birth dates, with configurable priority and a distinct visual marker.
- Adds a dedicated family part for former partners of biological, step, and social/adoptive parents, including date-aware partnership detection and relaxed-mode partner chains.
- Groups children of nephews and nieces by their relevant sibling or partner line and restores missing Degree labels for these paths.
- Adds optional latest occupation and residence details plus optional marriage date/place details to person boxes and partner entries.
- Adds configurable event-place sources for GEDCOM `PLAC`, the historical `_LOC` hierarchy at the event date, and the current `_LOC` hierarchy.
- Improves shared-place hierarchy display and summary-table spacing, and keeps Degree labels anchored in the upper-right corner of enriched person boxes.
- Adds compatibility support for webtrees 2.3 while retaining support for webtrees 2.2.6.
